tiny noise

A step sequencer. Fiddle with your own sound recordings and scanned drawings to maximize fun with both analog and digital noise.

Lanes of beeps, noise and your own recordings. Click cells or type numbers and patterns, draw pictures that become sound, save scenes, and write a score that plays by itself. Drawing mode includes spectrogram-style drawing and noise-based drawing (B/W and color).

Spectrogram and drawing

What you hear: time runs left to right (newest on the right, about 8.5 seconds shown), pitch runs bottom to top, brightness is loudness.

What you draw: paint on the black canvas and it becomes sound. Left to right is always time, and bright marks are loud. Choose how the rest is read under the canvas: Noise inks lets you paint with eight colours, each a different noise (thicker marks are louder, sprayed or speckled marks break the noise up, and a colour photo becomes a noise collage); Texture turns marks into grains of noise or of your recordings (dots become hits, lines become streams, scribbles become crackle, and up means grain size, tone, density or position in a recording), while Pitch reads the canvas like a spectrogram, with up meaning higher notes. Press Live scan and the drawing loops continuously, so every stroke is heard at once. Press and drag on the canvas to hold and scrub: the sound under your pointer sustains while you paint. The blue-green glow on the canvas is what actually comes out of the speakers as the playhead passes.

Files stay on your computer. A score remembers sample names, not the audio, so load the same files again before playing a score that uses them.

Cells cycle off, soft, medium, accent. The text box under each lane takes the same pattern as characters: . off, 1 soft, 2 medium, 3 or x accent. Its length sets Steps in loop (for example "x..x..x." is an 8-step loop). Every slider also has a number box you can type in. Noise and sample "frequency" is playback speed (1000 is normal). Record saves exactly what you hear. Live scan runs on its own, so you can paint and listen without starting the lanes; Start then adds them. Drawings are saved inside the score file, so they come back when you load it.
